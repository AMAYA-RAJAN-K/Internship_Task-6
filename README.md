#  **Sales Trend Analysis Using SQL Aggregations**

---

###  Objective  
The primary goal of this task is to perform time-based analysis on sales data to understand patterns in revenue generation and order volumes over different months and years. This helps in identifying seasonal trends, peak sales periods, and overall business growth trajectory.

---

###  Tools Used  
- **SQL (MySQL / PostgreSQL / SQLite)**  
- **MySQL Workbench** (for importing and querying data)  
- Dataset stored in: `online_sales_dataset` table

---

###  Dataset Summary  
The dataset contains records of customer transactions with attributes such as:
- Order identifiers
- Timestamps (order dates)
- Product quantities and prices
- Discount percentages
- Sales channel, priority, and other business metadata

---

###  Key Insights Derived

-  **Monthly Grouping** enabled the identification of sales patterns, showing which months consistently perform better in terms of revenue.
-  **Revenue Trends** revealed the impact of discounts on total earnings and allowed tracking of overall revenue growth or decline over time.
-  **Order Volume Trends** helped determine peak demand months and potential delivery/logistics pressure periods.
-  **Time Filtering** allowed focusing on specific business periods (e.g., calendar years) for more detailed and actionable insights.
-  **Chronological Sorting** ensured a clear timeline view, which is crucial for visualizing the progression or regression in performance.
-  **Top-Performing Months** by revenue helped highlight key campaigns, successful marketing seasons, or high-demand product cycles.

---

###  Business Value

- **Decision Making**: Identify the best months to launch promotions or scale back operations.
- **Inventory Planning**: Align stock levels with order trends to reduce overstock or shortages.
- **Marketing Strategy**: Optimize ad spend and campaigns around historically high-performing months.
- **KPI Benchmarking**: Establish monthly goals based on historical data trends.
